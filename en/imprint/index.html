Terms used on this website

We endeavor to use only correct terms on our website and in the services and certificates we provide. Nevertheless, some terms require explanation. Everything we offer relates exclusively to training and educating dogs so they can accompany people with proven mental-health conditions on airplanes and on journeys in general.

  • PSD — Psychiatric Service Dog (per US DOT) / Service Dog: the term used for dogs trained with a focus on flying and traveling.
  • Assistance Dog (PSD) — a synonym for “Service Dog”.
  • ESA — Emotional Support Animal: an animal (not necessarily a dog) demonstrably assigned to a specific person with a mental-health condition, without its own task training.

Declaration: “Service / Assistance Dog (PSD)”

We expressly point out that the term “service / assistance dog”, where used in our training content, must under no circumstances be confused with a state-recognized training course. We use the term as a translation and further development of “Psychiatric Service Dog”, documenting our goal of training dogs for people with mental-health conditions so that they are accepted by international airlines as “Service Dogs / Assistance Dogs”. Our training is designed to prepare dogs to fly and travel nationally and internationally as well as possible. Importantly, we always train human-animal teams — not dogs alone.
